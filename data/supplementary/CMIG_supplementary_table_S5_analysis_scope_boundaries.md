| Filtered Student-8+PL / D175 versus Student-8+PL70 / D129 fixed confidence filtering | Five-fold split-matched analysis | 5 folds; 200 paired case-fold observations, with cluster-aware sensitivity analyses | With matched training-validation assignments and identical evaluation cases, the fixed morphology-based filtering rule produced an observed Dice difference of -0.000934 whose two-way confidence interval crossed zero. | General failure of all confidence filtering methods. |
